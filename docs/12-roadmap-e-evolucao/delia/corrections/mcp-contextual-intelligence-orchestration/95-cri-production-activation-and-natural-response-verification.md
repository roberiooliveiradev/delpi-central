## 2. Root cause of the mechanical response

The incident response (`codigo_processo`, `nome_processo`,
`gestor_responsavel` UUID, `status_processo`, raw ISO `created_at`)
was generated by the **baseline deterministic render**, not by CRI:

- CRI was **OFF** in production: flag absent → empty injection →
  `fail_closed reason=invalid_env_value` → `decision=baseline` on every
  turn.
- Baseline path: model proposes a field selection;
  `_render_synthesis` renders it verbatim. In `detail`/`answer` modes,
  unmapped owner fields surface as raw `snake_case` labels and values
  stay verbatim — UUIDs and raw ISO timestamps included.
- Reproduced deterministically (`D1`): fixture mirroring the incident
  payload renders `Detalhes do registro <uuid>: - codigo_processo: …`
  — byte-similar to the production capture.
